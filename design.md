# Little White Rose — Design Direction

## Palette
- Background: `#0a0508` (near-black with deep wine undertone)
- Surface: `#130b0f` (cards, panels)
- Surface elevated: `#1e1018` (modals, elevated cards)
- Border: `#3a1528` (subtle rose border)
- Primary accent: `#c0395a` (deep rose red — CTAs, highlights)
- Secondary accent: `#e8678a` (lighter rose — hover states)
- Blush: `#f2b8cb` (soft pink — text accents, labels)
- Gold: `#d4a853` (premium feel — pricing, VIP labels)
- Text primary: `#f5e8ed` (warm off-white)
- Text muted: `#9a7080` (muted rose-gray)

## Typography
- Display: `Playfair Display` (bold serif, dramatic headlines)
- Body: `Lato` (clean, readable)
- Accent/tag: `Cormorant Garamond` (elegant italic)

## Layout
- Dark, intimate, editorial feel
- Bold headline cards inspired by Vesper template
- Overlapping content with rose glow effects
- Colorful content cards with distinct rose/blush/dark treatments
- Heavy use of full-bleed images with gradient overlays
- Asymmetric sections, not cookie-cutter grids

## Key Components
- Hero: full-height, model image, large bold serif name, blurred glow
- Content cards: image with bold title overlay (Vesper style), color-coded
- Lock overlay: blur + rose lock icon on gated content
- CTA buttons: deep rose, pill shape, slight glow on hover
- Chat UI: dark panel, rose message bubbles for AI replies
- Subscription cards: dark with gold border for premium tier

## Motion
- Staggered fade-in on page load
- Subtle scale on card hover
- Glow pulse on subscribe button
